# Checkpoint by SIP Guardian - 15 Slide Presentation Script

## Instructions for Creating in Google Slides / PowerPoint

**Design Tips:**
- Use FinLit Ventures brand colors if available, otherwise: Blue (#0ea5e9), Green (#22c55e), Red (#ef4444)
- Keep text minimal on slides, put details in speaker notes
- Use large fonts (24pt minimum for body, 48pt+ for titles)
- Include icons/emojis for visual interest
- Add screenshots of your actual prototype
- Keep each slide to max 3-4 bullet points

## PRESENTATION TIPS

**Delivery:**
- Practice timing: 10-12 minutes talk, 3-5 minutes Q&A
- Speak slowly and clearly
- Make eye contact with judges
- Show passion and confidence
- Refer to your working prototype often ("You can try this right now!")

**Handling Questions:**
- "Great question, let me show you in the live demo..."
- "That's in our PRD, I'd be happy to share details..."
- "We've thought about that—here's our approach..."
- For unknowns: "That's exactly what we're testing in shadow mode..."

**Key Messages to Repeat:**
1. "We intervene at the decision moment, not after"
2. "AI explains verified math, doesn't generate numbers"
3. "User always in control, we just inform"
4. "Validation-first: prove value before scaling"
5. "Working code, not mockups—try it yourself"

**Winning Statement:**
"We're not here with mockups and promises. We're here with working code, comprehensive strategy, and a validation framework. Give us 60 days and access to real data, and we'll prove whether this works. That's all we're asking. Data-driven decisions, not hype."
